Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026. (한국어 버전은 아래에 있습니다 / Korean version below.)

This policy explains how personal data is collected and used by the applications provided by vivid_wave / Geungho Lee (the "Developer"), including Vintage Pomodoro (Windows), PomodoroCage / Auto Pomodoro (iOS), RiseSync, 사계기록 (Harugirok), Brainwire, and 35Closer (collectively, the "Apps").

1. Information We Collect

Data handling differs by app and by the optional features a user enables. Timer history and settings are stored on the device by default. When Auto Pomodoro's account sync, cross-device timer sync, AI interpretation, or feedback features are used, the app sends the information described below to the services needed for that feature.

  • On-device data: focus and break session history, timer and lock settings, and app preferences are stored locally for app features.
  • Auto Pomodoro account sync: When the user signs in or enables account sync, account identifiers and authentication data, plus the timer/session snapshot selected for sync, are sent to the Brainwire service at brainwire-f2gf.onrender.com. This is an internet service, not a LAN-only connection.
  • Auto Pomodoro timer sync: When configured, the app sends the account name, four-digit PIN, and timer-state payload to a Cloudflare Worker at cloud-account-storage.imdisablebutgodisable.workers.dev so the user's devices can exchange timer state.
  • AI interpretation: If the user asks the AI feature to interpret a message, the message and recent conversation history are sent to the Brainwire service, which routes the request to an AI provider. The provider may process the request under its own terms. Provider-specific retention is not controlled by the app and has not been established here; do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in AI messages.
  • Feedback: When feedback is submitted, the feedback text and app/OS diagnostic details are sent to the support service. Optional contact information, screenshots, or recent chat context are sent only when the user selects and submits them.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Running the focus/break timer and displaying today's session statistics.
  • Syncing account data and timer state across the user's own devices when the user enables the relevant Auto Pomodoro feature.
  • Routing user-requested AI interpretation and responding to submitted support feedback.
  • Optional break-lock features (e.g. Vintage Pomodoro's strict lock) that keep the app active during a scheduled break — this setting is stored locally and never leaves the device.

3. Storage and Deletion

On-device data is removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to the operating system's backup behavior. Data sent for Auto Pomodoro sync or support is processed by the named services; uninstalling the app does not delete server-side copies. The app does not publish a verified retention period for those services. Contact the Developer below to ask about a deletion request.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Auto Pomodoro sends feature data to the Brainwire service and Cloudflare Worker described above. AI interpretation requests are routed by Brainwire to an AI provider; the provider may process those requests to return a response. These transfers support the feature requested by the user. No advertising or cross-app tracking is used by Auto Pomodoro.

5. Your Rights

You may uninstall any app at any time to remove all data stored on your device. For any other questions, contact the email address below.

  • Auto Pomodoro 타이머 동기화: 설정된 경우 기기 간 타이머 상태를 교환하기 위해 계정 이름, 4자리 PIN, 타이머 상태 데이터가 Cloudflare Worker(cloud-account-storage.imdisablebutgodisable.workers.dev)로 전송됩니다.
